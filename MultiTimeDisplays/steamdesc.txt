[h1]Unified Rate Formatting[/h1]

this mod looks for all occurences of "per second" and "per cycle" and unifies their measurement display to include both variants

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sgt-Imalas/Sgt_Imalas-Oni-Mods/refs/heads/master/Compat_All.png[/img]
[h1]Current latest Version: 1.0[/h1]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Features[/h1]
[list][*] "Per Second"" measurements are now displayed as "Per Second (Per Cycle)".
[*] "Per Cycle"" measurements are now displayed as "(Per Second) Per Cycle".
[*] Unified Rate Converters:[list]
	[*] Mass
	[*] Units (note: per second display might get rounded to 0 for small perCycle numbers)
	[*] Calories (note: per second display might get rounded to 0 for small perCycle numbers)
	[*] Rads Emission info of elements[/list]
[/list]

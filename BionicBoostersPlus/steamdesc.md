[h1]Bionics Expanded[/h1]

Adds new unique boosters, bionic skills and an expanded progression in booster creation, making bionics more versatile in different environments.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sgt-Imalas/Sgt_Imalas-Oni-Mods/refs/heads/master/Compat_All_DLC3_Required.png[/img]

[h1]Current latest Version: 1.0[/h1]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]New Buildings:[/h1]
[list][*] "Booster 'Recycling' Station: allows you to break down boosters back into microchips (yield may vary)
[*] Experimental Laser Soldering Station: enables crafting of higher tier, "fused" boosters that elevate bionics to new levels of performance (at a cost...)
[/list]
[h1]New Skills:[/h1]
[list][*] Internal Plating: the bionic duplicant becomes more resistant to tissue damage
[*] Optimized Circuitry: reduces the overal power draw of the bionic duplicant and reduces their overal stress
[/list]
[h1]New Boosters:[/h1]
[list][*] "Fused" Boosters: these new type of boosters offer a higher stat bonus than normal boosters at a sanity cost (+stress). Crafted by combining two of the base boosters at the laser soldering station.
[*] Dreaming Booster: allows bionic duplicants to dream (of electric sheep?) to produce dream journals. Has power draw while active.
[*] Battery Slot Booster: trades one of your booster slots for an additional power bank slot
[*] Waterproofing Booster: eliminates most stress generated by water exposure by coating the internals in an extra thick layer of gear oil (slight increase to gear oil consumption, negates 90% of water stress)
[*] Biomechanical Repair Booster: allows the duplicant to self repair (heal) and to purge rads. Has power draw while active.
[*] Subdermal Solar Booster: grants the ability to gain joules by standing in light. (can recharge powerbanks that support it if light is strong enough).
[/list]

[h3] Update Loop / Mod marked as incompatible?[/h3]

Either the game failed its mod extraction process, or your OneDrive is breaking folder access, preventing the game from writing mod files.
Turn it off for your documents folder as described in [url=https://support.klei.com/hc/en-us/articles/360029880551-Oxygen-Not-Included-Troubleshooting-Guide]Klei's Troubleshooter guide[/url] and force update the mod with Mod Updater

you can also grab a direct download on [url=https://github.com/Sgt-Imalas/Sgt_Imalas-Oni-Mods/releases]GitHub[/url] to get the latest version
